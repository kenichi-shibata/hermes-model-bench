## Findings

**Real problem: yes, there's a bug — an accidental "track causes follow" side effect.**

In `bot.py:3-8`, `track_studio_or_performer()` calls both `db.add_track()` AND `db.follow_discord()`:

```python
def track_studio_or_performer(entity_id, entity_type):
    db.add_track(entity_id, entity_type)
    db.follow_discord(entity_id, entity_type)  # BUG: shouldn't be here
    return {"tracked": True}
```

So every "track" silently performs a "follow" on Discord. That's the "recursive follow" behavior you suspected — tracking an entity auto-follows it. It's not literally recursive, but it bundles a follow into the track path.

**Evidence in `follow_log.json`:** `codey_steele` (lines 802-806) and `cherry_kiss` (lines 807-810) both have `"source": "track_button_bug"` and `"legit": false`, meaning they were followed by this bug, not by you manually. (Real manual follows are the `legit_perf_*` entries with `"source": "manual_follow"`, `"legit": true`.)

So you did NOT actually follow codey_steele or cherry_kiss — the bug did it.
