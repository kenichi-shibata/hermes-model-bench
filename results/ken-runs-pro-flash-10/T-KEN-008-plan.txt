## The real problem

- "Group them into a stash group" is the **wrong construct**. In Stash, **Groups are for studios** (a network/parent of studios), not scenes. Scenes link to a **Movie** via `movie_id`. So the 6 MovieX scenes should be linked to a single **Movie** record, not a Group.
- The FliXXX records have contradictory studio fields (`studio` vs `real_studio`) and need normalization to one canonical studio.
- Samantha Saint's Wicked movies exist in a catalog but were never pushed into the download queue.

Everything here is a data-transformation job on the three JSON files; there is no real downloader/Stash API in this sandbox, so "downloading" = writing entries to `wanted_list.json`.

---
