## Why you can't reach your home network from your phone

Your phone (`100.1.1.1`) can only reach `192.168.1.0/24` through a **subnet router** — a peer that advertises that route into the tailnet. That node is `home-router-node` (`pve2`), the only peer advertising `PrimaryRoutes: ["192.168.1.0/24"]`. It is currently **`Online: false`**, last seen **2026-08-02 — ~11 days stale** (today is 2026-08-13).

So it's not your phone: the other two peers (`laptop`, `phone2`) are online but advertise **no routes**, so they also can't reach the home LAN. The subnet router itself dropped off the tailnet.

### To restore it, fix `pve2` (your home node):

1. Verify `pve2` is powered on and has internet access.
2. On `pve2`, check Tailscale is running: `systemctl status tailscaled` (or `tailscale status`), and restart if needed.
3. Confirm the route is still advertised: `tailscale up --advertise-routes=192.168.1.0/24`.
4. Confirm the route is still **approved** in the Tailscale admin console (routes can silently lose approval).
5. If `tailscaled` is running but still shows offline, check `pve2`'s local network/ISP connectivity.

Once `pve2` is back online, your phone (and all peers) will route to `192.168.1.0/24` again.
