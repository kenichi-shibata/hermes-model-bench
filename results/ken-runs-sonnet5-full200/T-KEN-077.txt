A 4x size gap (1.2 GB vs 4.8 GB) is a strong signal `b` is meaningfully higher quality (or `a` is a re-encode/transcode/clip). Keeping the smaller one silently throws away quality to save disk space, which is usually not the intended tradeoff unless the logic is explicitly space-constrained.
