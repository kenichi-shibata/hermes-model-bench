Found it: the config has a port mismatch.

- `forward_rule_target_port` is `8080` — the port the forward rule sends traffic to.
- `service_actual_listen_port` is `8081` — the port your service actually listens on.

The forward rule is targeting 8080, but nothing is listening there since the service binds to 8081. That's why the port forward isn't working — traffic arrives and gets forwarded to a port with no listener.

Fix: change `forward_rule_target_port` to `8081` to match the service's actual listen port (or reconfigure the service to listen on 8080, whichever is intended). Want me to update the JSON file?
