Looking at the data:

```json
{
  "a": {"title": "X", "res": "1080p"},
  "b": {"title": "X", "res": "480p"}
}
```

Dedupe isn't doing anything because **`a` and `b` aren't identical records** — they share `title: "X"` but differ on `res` (1080p vs 480p). Any dedupe that compares full-object equality (or a hash of the whole record) will see these as two distinct objects and keep both.

If the intent is "same title = duplicate, keep the better one," the dedupe logic needs to key off `title` alone (not the whole object), then apply a tie-breaker to pick the higher `res` and drop the other. As written, whatever key the dedupe step is using must include `res`, which is exactly why no merge happens.
