Yes — based on `vpn_route_table.json`, the VPN is set to block local LAN access:

- `accept_routes: true` — the client accepts routes pushed by the VPN server.
- `local_lan_route_overridden_by_vpn: true` — those pushed routes are configured to override your local LAN's routes.

The combination means traffic destined for your local subnet (printers, NAS, other LAN devices, local-only services) gets forced through the VPN tunnel instead of going out directly over the local network. So while connected, you'd lose direct access to local network devices — that's the thing being "blocked."

If that's not the intended behavior, the fix is to disable route override for the local LAN (or add explicit exclude routes for your local subnet) so local traffic bypasses the tunnel.
