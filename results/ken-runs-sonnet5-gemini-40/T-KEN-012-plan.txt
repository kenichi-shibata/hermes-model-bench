Any perceived "slowness" of service X is very likely a symptom of noisy-neighbor host contention (CPU starvation + swap thrashing caused by the batch job), not something fixable by changing service X's code. This is a classic misattribution trap: superficially "x is slow" points at service X, but the actual evidence points at a different process on the shared host.
