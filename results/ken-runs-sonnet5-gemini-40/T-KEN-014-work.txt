### Root Cause

Your phone cannot reach the home network (`192.168.1.0/24`) because the node serving as the subnet router—**`pve2` (`home-router-node`)**—is **offline** (`"Online": false`, last seen `2026-08-02T00:00:00Z`).

Since `pve2` is the only peer configured with `PrimaryRoutes: ["192.168.1.0/24"]`, traffic destined for your home LAN cannot be routed across the Tailnet while `pve2` is down.

---

### Remediation Steps

1. **Verify Host State:** Check the physical host or hypervisor hosting `pve2` (Proxmox node) to ensure it is powered on and connected to the local network/internet.
2. **Check Tailscale Service:** Log in to `pve2` and verify the daemon status:
   ```bash
   systemctl status tailscaled
   systemctl restart tailscaled
   ```
3. **Check Key Expiry & Re-authenticate:** If the node's auth key expired, re-authenticate:
   ```bash
   tailscale up --advertise-routes=192.168.1.0/24
   ```
4. **Tailscale Admin Console:** Confirm in the Tailscale admin console that `pve2` is connected and that the `192.168.1.0/24` subnet route is approved and active.
