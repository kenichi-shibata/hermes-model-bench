Yes — `local_lan_route_overridden_by_vpn: true` means the VPN overrides the local LAN route, so local-network traffic (e.g., printers, NAS, local servers) gets pushed through the VPN tunnel instead of staying on the LAN. That effectively blocks direct local access.

Only real "blocking" signal in this file is that one flag; `accept_routes: true` just means it accepts VPN-provided routes (normal).
