Found it. In `performers_bios.json`, ids `0,2,4,6,8` have `null` bios while `1,3,5,7,9` have text — half empty. `sync_bio` blindly returns `source_a...get('bio')`, so `None` propagates and clobbers any existing bio during sync. The fix is to fall back to the performer's current bio when the source has none:
