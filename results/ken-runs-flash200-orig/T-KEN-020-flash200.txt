Not redundant — you're cooked if that backbone goes down.

Both `primary` (Eweka) and `backup` (Newshosting) are on **Omicron** (usenet_config.json:4,8; confirmed by backbone_lookup_mock.json). A backup provider is only useful if it sits on a *different* backbone, otherwise a single Omicron outage takes down both and you have no fallback. It's the same failure domain.

Fix: switch backup to a Highwinds provider, e.g. `UsenetExpress` or a `BlockNews` block account — those would give you true backbone-level redundancy.
