`bulk_delete` now snapshots each record into a `backup_store` before hard-deleting; `rollback_bulk_delete(backup_store, db)` restores them. On any exception mid-loop, the partial deletes are rolled back automatically before re-raising. Callers can pass a persistent store (e.g. a file-backed dict) instead of the default in-memory one, and it assumes `db.get(id)` and `db.restore(id, record)` exist alongside `hard_delete`.
