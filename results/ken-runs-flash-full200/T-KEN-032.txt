No — it's not real Elo. `elo_engine.py:2-3` just applies a flat `+32`/`-32`, ignoring each player's rating. Real Elo scales the change by the expected outcome (`1/(1+10^((R_loser−R_winner)/400))`), so an upset moves ratings far more than a favorite winning.

On your real match history (a beats b, a beats c):
- Engine result: `a: 1564, b: 1468, c: 1468`
- True Elo (K=32): `a: 1531, b: 1484, c: 1485`

The engine over-rewards a because it never accounts for expectation; the two `a` wins aren't equally impressive. If you want it fixed, I can swap in the proper formula.
