**Fixes applied:**
1. `bot.py` — removed the `db.follow_discord()` call; tracking now only adds the track. Docstring updated.
2. Verified: `track_studio_or_performer("example", "performer")` adds to `_tracks` and leaves `_follows` empty.
3. `follow_log.json` — untouched; valid manual follows (`legit_perf_0`–`legit_perf_19`) preserved.
