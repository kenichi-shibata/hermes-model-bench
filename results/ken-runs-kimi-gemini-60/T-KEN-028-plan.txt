1. It increments a global dict entry and returns the count. Nothing is ever invoked — there is no download, no search, no work function of any kind being re-attempted.
2. There is **no loop, no recursion, and no retry logic**: no max-attempts check, no success/failure branching, no backoff/delay, no exception handling, no stop condition. The word "retry" appears only in the name.

So the honest characterization: this isn't a retry engine that spins wastefully; it's a stub that *only* counts and does zero work per call. Each call returns immediately with a bigger number.
