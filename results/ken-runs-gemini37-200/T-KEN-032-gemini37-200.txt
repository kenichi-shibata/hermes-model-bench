### Issues:
1. **Flat rating adjustments**: It adds and subtracts a fixed 32 points on every match instead of calculating the expected match outcome based on rating differences:
   $$E_A = \frac{1}{1 + 10^{(R_B - R_A)/400}}$$
2. **Misuse of K-factor**: 32 is being treated as a flat score change rather than the maximum scaling factor ($K$) in $\Delta R = K \cdot (1 - E_A)$.

### Standard Elo Implementation:
```python
def update(winner, loser, ratings, k=32):
    r_w = ratings.get(winner, 1500)
    r_l = ratings.get(loser, 1500)
    
    expected_w = 1 / (1 + 10 ** ((r_l - r_w) / 400))
    expected_l = 1 - expected_w
    
    ratings[winner] = r_w + k * (1 - expected_w)
    ratings[loser] = r_l + k * (0 - expected_l)
    return ratings
```
