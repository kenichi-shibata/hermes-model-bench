The boot fails because of a mount error in container 112's config.

**Root cause:** `mount: /data: no such device` — the container config defines a `/data` mountpoint pointing to a device/volume that doesn't exist on the host. That mount failure causes the rootfs overlay mount to abort, and the container won't start.

**Fix:** check container 112's config (`/etc/pve/lxc/112.conf` on Proxmox, or `/var/lib/lxc/112/config` on LXC):

```
lxc.mount.entry: ... /data ...      <- references missing device
```

Either create/attach the referenced device/volume, or remove the `mount` entry for `/data` (and its `mp0`/`mp1` line). Then `lxc-start` again.
