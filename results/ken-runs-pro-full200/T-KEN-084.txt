The service itself is fine (40ms latency), but the host shows **60% CPU steal** — that's your problem.

CPU steal time means the hypervisor is taking CPU cycles away from your VM to give to other tenants on an oversubscribed physical host. 60% steal is severe: your VM is effectively only getting ~40% of the CPU it expects, so everything (including `x`) runs slow regardless of the app's own latency.

Recommended fixes:
- Migrate the VM to a less oversubscribed host (or ask your cloud provider to).
- Check for a noisy neighbor / right-size the instance, or switch to a dedicated/CPU-pinned instance type.
- Investigate burstable instance CPU credit exhaustion if this is AWS T2/T3 or similar.
