Not redundant. Both providers are on the Omicron backbone, so you're cooked if Omicron goes down.

- `Eweka` → Omicron
- `Newshosting` → Omicron

Same backbone = same upstream. The "backup" only protects against a provider-specific outage, not a backbone failure. To be truly redundant, add a provider on a different backbone (e.g. `UsenetExpress` or `BlockNews` → Highwinds).
