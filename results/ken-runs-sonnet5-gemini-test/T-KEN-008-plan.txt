There is no runtime, release date, or scene count in the catalog to disambiguate which of the 12 titles "MovieX" actually is. **Do not guess-assign it to one of the 12 titles** — that would silently corrupt metadata.

1. Add a new file `groups.json` (or a `"groups"` array in `scenes_db.json`) with a schema like:
