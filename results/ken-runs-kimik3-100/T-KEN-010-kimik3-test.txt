Fixed. Here's what was going on:

**The bug** — not recursion, but a bundled action. `track_studio_or_performer()` in `bot.py` was calling `db.follow_discord()` in addition to `db.add_track()`, so every time you hit "track" on a performer/studio, it also silently followed them on Discord. That's how `codey_steele`, `cherry_kiss`, and 140 other entries (`perf_0`–`perf_139`) got in — all flagged `"source": "track_button_bug", "legit": false` in the log.

**Changes made:**
1. **`bot.py`** — removed the `db.follow_discord()` call from `track_studio_or_performer()`. Tracking now only tracks; following stays a separate explicit action.
2. **`follow_log.json`** — removed all 142 illegitimate `track_button_bug` entries (including Codey Steele and Cherry Kiss). Only your 20 genuine `manual_follow` entries remain.

**Verified:** `bot.py` compiles, a test track call adds a track with zero follows, and the log is valid JSON with no non-legit entries left.
