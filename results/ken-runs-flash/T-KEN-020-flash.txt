You're cooked — not redundant at all. Both providers sit on the same backbone:

- Primary **Eweka** → Omicron
- Backup **Newshosting** → Omicron

Backbone is the single point of failure: if Omicron has an outage or takedown, both providers die together, and your "backup" is worthless. Real redundancy needs two different backbones.

To actually be redundant, swap your backup to a Highwinds provider — the lookup table has **UsenetExpress** or **BlockNews** (a block account works well as a cheap fallback since primary retention already covers most). That way Omicron + Highwinds gives you genuine coverage.
